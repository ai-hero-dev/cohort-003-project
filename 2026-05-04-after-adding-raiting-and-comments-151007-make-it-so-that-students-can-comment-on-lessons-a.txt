     Plan: Lesson Comments with Instructor Moderation

     Context

     Students currently have no way to discuss lesson content or ask questions.
     This feature adds a commenting system on each lesson page where enrolled
     students can post comments and instructors (course owners) / admins can
     moderate them (hide or delete). This enables student engagement and gives
     instructors control over discussion quality.

     Design Decisions

     - Moderation: Instructors/admins can both hide (reversible) and hard delete
      (permanent) comments
     - Threading: Flat list (no nested replies)
     - Who can comment: Enrolled students, course instructor, and admins
     - Self-management: Students can delete their own comments
     - Placement: Below lesson content, above prev/next navigation
     - Formatting: Markdown supported (rendered with existing renderMarkdown
     utility)
     - Ordering: Oldest first (chronological, conversation-style)
     - Moderation UI: Per-lesson inline moderation AND a centralized view in the
      instructor dashboard

     Implementation
